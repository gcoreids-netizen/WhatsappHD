Why WhatsApp Blurs Status

1
Exact 1080 × 1920 Resolution
WhatsApp downscales oversized 4K photos aggressively. Pre-scaling to exact 1080p stops WhatsApp's harsh resizer from smearing fine details.
2
Stay in the File Size Sweet Spot
Keep photos under 1.2 MB and videos at 2.5–4 Mbps. Files in this range pass through WhatsApp's servers with minimal re-encoding.
3
Pre-Sharpen & Toggle "HD" on Upload
Our anti-blur unsharp mask compensates for WhatsApp's slight softening. When posting on WhatsApp Status, always tap the HD icon at the top if available.
